Project Description

We decided to make a parking application for the University of Central Florida which is both different from the UCF parking widget found on myucf.edu and an improvement on it. We found through talking amongst ourselves as well as talking to other students that many had problems with the design and functionality of the already-existing widget, so we made an application to streamline the process of students finding parking.
